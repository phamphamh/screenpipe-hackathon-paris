METHODS WE BUILD ON
(sources checked on 17/10, details in labs/phamphamh/recherche/)

CONSULTING FIRMS

- McKinsey — Pyramid Principle (Barbara Minto) and 7-step problem solving: answer first, break the problem down, prioritize.
  → Our audit opens on one number (€71,700 a year), then ranks 19 repeated tasks and keeps the 5 worth automating.
  https://www.barbaraminto.com/ · https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/how-to-master-the-seven-step-problem-solving-process

- Pareto 80/20 (Joseph Juran): the vital few, the trivial many.
  → Our 80/20 filter: 55,510 captures become 14,470 work moments.

- BCG — the 10/20/70 rule: 10% algorithms, 20% technology and data, 70% people and processes.
  → We start from what people actually do, not from the tool.
  https://www.bcg.com/featured-insights/the-leaders-guide-to-transforming-with-ai

- BCG × Harvard — "Jagged frontier" (758 consultants, 2023): inside the AI frontier, +12.2% tasks done, 25.1% faster, 40%+ better quality; outside it, 19 points fewer correct answers.
  → Knowing WHICH task to give to AI is the job.
  https://mitsloan.mit.edu/sites/default/files/2023-10/SSRN-id4573321.pdf

- Bain — Results Delivery: only 12% of big change programs fully reach their goals (184 companies).
  → Every euro in the audit has a task, a person and a workflow attached.
  https://www.bain.com/insights/results-delivery-busting-3-common-change-management-myths

- Big Four — KPMG Trusted AI, EY AI Confidence Index, Deloitte Trustworthy AI: inventory, risks, controls.
  → Every agent action is logged with a screenshot.
  https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/pdf/2025/trusted-ai-controls-matrix-tool-us.pdf

- Lean / Toyota — gemba ("go see the real work"), value stream mapping, the 8 wastes.
  → screenpipe is a digital gemba: we see the work where it happens.
  https://www.lean.org/lexicon-terms/value-stream-mapping/

- Process & task mining — Celonis, Soroco, Skan: time per task, app switches, copy-paste, measured from traces.
  → Same idea, without installing enterprise spyware: screenpipe stays on the laptop.
  https://www.celonis.com/insights/topics/what-is-task-mining

AI LABS

- OpenAI × UPenn — "GPTs are GPTs" (Eloundou et al., 2023): a task is exposed if AI cuts its time by 50%+ at equal quality. E1 = a chatbot is enough; E2 = it needs software built on an LLM. 47–56% of tasks with E2 tools.
  → E1 means training, E2 means a workflow. We deploy the E2 part.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10130

- Anthropic Economic Index (Sept 2025): businesses using the API automate 77% of the time. Complex deployments are held back more by access to context than by the model.
  → That's our thesis: the problem is the context, not the data.
  https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-september-2025-report

- Anthropic — productivity gains (Nov 2025): ~80% time saved per task over 100,000 conversations, but time spent outside the chat isn't counted.
  → screenpipe sees that time.
  https://www.anthropic.com/research/estimating-productivity-gains

- Stanford — WORKBank (2025): cross what workers want automated with what AI can do. Workers want 46.1% of their tasks automated; 41% of YC startup use cases land where workers don't want it.
  → The employee validates their own tasks before anything is shared.
  https://arxiv.org/html/2506.06576v2

- Stanford — WONDERBREAD (2024): real recorded back-office workflows on a Magento store.
  → Our Atelier Nova data, and the requests our agents run live.
